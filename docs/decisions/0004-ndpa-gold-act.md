# 0004 - NDPA as the gold Act

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 5 August 2026

## Context

OpenActs needs one instrument to prove Source acquisition, canonical structure, Provision traversal, exact lookup, public rendering, verification, and correction. The Constitution is a compelling public flagship, but using it first would combine the basic corpus test with constitutional structure, amendment, and currentness questions.

## Decision

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 is the first manually structured, source-verified gold Act. It is used to validate the OpenActs data model and known-law reader, not to prove automated extraction or broad corpus coverage.

The Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria remains an early follow-on priority after the gold-Act workflow is reliable. Its addition is a deliberate second-stage test of constitutional structure, source provenance, and links to separately enacted amendment instruments, not an unspecified backlog item.

## Why

NDPA provides enough real legal structure and public relevance to exercise the product while keeping the first test bounded. That allows failures to expose problems in OpenActs rather than being confused with the exceptional complexity of the Constitution. Once the workflow is trustworthy, the Constitution becomes a stronger and safer flagship expansion.
